𓂀 What Is Egyptian Religion?

Ancient Egyptian Religion, also called Kemetic Spirituality, is one of the oldest sacred traditions in the world. It honors divine forces called Neteru, including powerful deities like Isis (Auset), Osiris, Ra, Hathor, Anubis, Bastet and Thoth. The goddess Isis/Auset represents healing, divine motherhood, protection, wisdom, magic and sacred feminine power. Egyptian spirituality focuses on balance, truth, manifestation, spiritual growth, energy alignment, devotion and sacred ritual practices.
